# Privacy Policy for MacPacker

MacPacker does not collect any personal information, usage information or IPs or use any tracking technologies.

The one exception is the newsletter on the welcome window, and only if you use it. When you enter your email address and press Subscribe, MacPacker sends that address to Gumroad, Inc., which runs the mailing list. Gumroad emails you once to confirm, and you are subscribed only after you confirm. Every newsletter email contains an unsubscribe link. MacPacker keeps no copy of the address. Gumroad processes it under its own privacy policy: https://gumroad.com/privacy
